Frequently asked questions

Everyday questions, answered.

The app currently uses German labels. Below, each quoted button label is followed by its English meaning.

Does scanning save an opening date?

The opening date is only saved when you tap “Als geöffnet speichern” (Save as opened). You can change the name, date and time first. Cancelling a scan or draft does not create an entry.

How can I enter a different date?

Before saving, tap the opening date and choose the correct day and time. You cannot choose a time in the future.

If a date is already saved, open “Neue Packung geöffnet” (Opened a new package), choose the date you want and save again. This replaces the previous entry for that barcode.

What happens when I choose “Neue Packung geöffnet” (Opened a new package)?

You can save a new opening date for the same barcode. The new entry only replaces the old one after you confirm. Choosing “Abbrechen” (Cancel) keeps the previous date.

Gone Sour keeps one entry per barcode. It cannot track several open packages of the same product separately.

Does Gone Sour work offline?

You can view saved products offline. You can also save a new opening date without an internet connection by entering a valid barcode and product name yourself. Only the automatic lookup of product names and images from Open Food Facts needs an internet connection.

Why can’t the app find my product?

The external Open Food Facts database does not include every product. A missing internet connection can also prevent a lookup. You can enter the name yourself and still save the product. If you enter a barcode manually, check the number below it; EAN-8, EAN-13 and UPC-A are supported.

How long are my entries kept?

Entries are deleted two calendar months after you last explicitly saved them, the next time you use the app. Looking up an entry again does not extend the period. Saving a new opening date restarts it for that barcode.

Can the app tell me whether food is still good?

Gone Sour reminds you of the opening date you saved. The app does not assess a food’s shelf life or safety. Follow the instructions on the packaging and consider storage conditions and the state of the product.

Where can I find information about my data?

In the app, the information icon on the home screen opens the privacy notice. On this website, you can find it under Privacy, including details about automatic product lookups through Open Food Facts.
