What the app stores locally

When you explicitly save an opening, Gone Sour records the barcode, product name, any downloaded product image, opening date and the time you saved the entry. The app stores this data locally on your iPhone. There is no account and no server of our own for your products.

A scan or a cancelled draft does not create a saved opening entry. Viewing an existing entry again does not extend its retention period.

Retention and backups

Two calendar months after you last explicitly saved an entry, it is deleted the next time you use the app. A newly confirmed save for the same barcode replaces the previous entry and starts a new retention period.

Depending on your iOS settings, the data may be included in device backups. The retention and deletion of these backups depend on your backup settings and the backup service you use.

Camera and barcodes

With your camera permission, the app reads a barcode on your device. Gone Sour does not store or transmit camera images. You can also enter a barcode manually and change camera permission in iOS settings.

The connection to Open Food Facts

If there is no saved product for a barcode, Gone Sour automatically looks up product information on Open Food Facts. Open Food Facts receives the requested barcode, your IP address and technically necessary connection data. The app also downloads available product images from Open Food Facts.

Your opening date and product names you enter yourself are not sent to Open Food Facts. The app can display a previously saved opening locally without starting another product lookup.

Processing and retention by this independent service are governed by the Open Food Facts privacy policy. For IP addresses and visitor logs, Open Food Facts lists security purposes, technical analysis and usage measurement, as well as a retention period of three years. This general statement by the service concerns Open Food Facts; it is separate from the two-month retention period for your products in Gone Sour.

Purposes and legal bases

Local processing allows opening dates to be saved and displayed again at your request. The lookup on Open Food Facts adds product names and images for the barcode you enter or scan. Where this involves processing personal data and the processing is necessary to provide these features you have requested, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR.

The legal basis for providing and securing this website is Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR. The legitimate interest is to make help and privacy information reliably available and to prevent abuse and technical attacks. The linked privacy policies of GitHub and Open Food Facts describe their independent processing.

Camera access is an operating system permission that you can change in iOS settings. It does not constitute blanket consent to other data processing. Barcodes can also be entered without camera permission. There is no legal obligation to record products, grant camera access or submit support enquiries.

No advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs

Gone Sour contains no advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs. You do not need to register to use the app. The app does not send your saved openings to the provider.
